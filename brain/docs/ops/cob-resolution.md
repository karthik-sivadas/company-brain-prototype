# COB conflict resolution

A coordination-of-benefits (COB) conflict is raised when two active plans disagree on
primary/secondary status, or on remaining frequency for a CDT code.

1. The system never auto-writes to the PMS on a COB conflict.
2. An exception card is pre-filled with both payer extracts and a suggested primary payer.
3. A Kochi RCM associate confirms or overrides during the 4 PM – 1 AM IST shift.
4. Anything still ambiguous after review escalates to the RCM lead for sign-off.
